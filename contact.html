What to include with a correction

Tell us which page was affected, what was inaccurate, and—when available—the current document published by the relevant carrier. Avoid sending names, addresses, tracking numbers, payment information, or sensitive shipment details. We cannot investigate lost parcels or process carrier claims.

Editorial scope

ParcelProof cannot provide carrier support, emergency handling instructions, customs clearance services, parcel pickup or price quotes. Contact the carrier or relevant authority for case-specific shipment questions. You can also read our about page and privacy notice.
