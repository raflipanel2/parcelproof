Our approach / the real-world difference

A label can't rescue a bad pack.

Carrier instructions often start with an ordinary but crucial detail: use a strong box and prevent the contents from shifting. We turn that advice into concrete checks you can perform at your own table.

We separate protective padding from box strength, explain why a lightweight oversized parcel can cost more, and flag items that require current carrier-specific eligibility checks. No single rule fits every product or transport route.
