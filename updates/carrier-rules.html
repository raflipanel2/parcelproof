What to verify before buying a shipping label

A pre-purchase audit of dimensions, service eligibility, restricted goods, fees and documentation.

ParcelProof EditorialReviewed 11 Nov 2026Not a live rules feed

Published packing examples can remain useful for years, while service terms, fees and acceptance rules may change much more quickly. The goal of this note is to teach you which facts require a live check before each shipment.

Service and route first

Identify the carrier, exact service name, origin, destination and whether the route is domestic or cross-border. Do not copy a rule from an international product to a domestic product simply because both use the same company.

Save or bookmark the actual service page you relied on. A product may be accepted on one route or service and restricted on another.

Measure the parcel you will actually tender

Enter exterior dimensions of the finished sealed box and gross scale weight. Size limits and dimensional-weight factors vary; some services also charge additional handling fees for shape, length or oversized packages.

Ask for the full quote from the shipping provider. A dimensional-weight calculator alone does not know all rate thresholds and surcharges.

Check contents eligibility independently

Lithium batteries, aerosols, flammable liquids, powders and other regulated goods require exact classification. Common consumer products can still have shipping restrictions.

Do not conceal risky items or substitute a decorative marking for a legally required label. If contents or battery condition are uncertain, stop and ask the selected carrier.

Confirm the paperwork and label

International shipments may need customs data, correct item values and classification. Returns may require authorization numbers or specific documentation.

Verify the address, label date and service type just before tendering. Old barcodes must be removed from reused boxes.

Keep a dated source record

Record the source URL and the date on which you reviewed the requirement. If a carrier updates its policy, this lets you identify which advice might need revisiting.

Our site is not connected to a live carrier rule API. The source directory is a starting point, not a replacement for the current published terms.

Responsible next step: Treat a quote and service eligibility check obtained for the actual parcel and date as authoritative over general examples in an independent guide.
