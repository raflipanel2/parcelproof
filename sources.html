Sources and further reading

The references below are useful starting points for packaging method, dimensional weight and mailing requirements. They may be revised by their publisher. ParcelProof is not affiliated with or endorsed by these organizations.

Packaging and cushioning

  • USPS — How to prepare and send a package. Box quality, old markings, cushioning and closure.
  • USPS Postal Explorer — Preparing packages. General packaging, fragile objects, labels and sealing.
  • FedEx — How to pack breakables. Separation, double boxing and packaging steps for delicate items.
  • UPS — Packaging tips. Carton choice, label placement and basic protection.

Dimensions, ratings and shipping restrictions

  • UPS — Shipping dimensions and weight. Dimensional weight, measurement and illustrative service considerations.
  • UPS — How to ship a package. General parcel preparation and labeling workflow.
  • USPS Postal Explorer. Current domestic and international mailing standards, including restrictions.

How to interpret these links

We do not combine these organizations' published recommendations into a mandatory universal packing standard. For example, cushioning guidance can differ with shipment type and fragile goods. Your carrier, service agreement and item properties take precedence. If you spot an outdated source link, please contact us.
