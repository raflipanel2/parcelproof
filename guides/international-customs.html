International parcels: packaging and customs information

Separate physical packing from declarations, import rules and route-specific restrictions.

International shipping adds rules that a domestic parcel may never encounter. Even a sound physical pack can be delayed or rejected when a declaration, origin detail or eligibility requirement is missing.

ParcelProof EditorialReviewed 11 Nov 2026Independent educational guide

Confirm that the goods may cross the border

Check the origin country, destination country, carrier service and actual product type. Some goods are prohibited, some require licenses, and others require special handling or documentation.

Do not infer permission from the fact that an online checkout permits label creation. A carrier’s acceptance and a destination customs authority’s admissibility can be different questions.

Describe contents accurately

A customs declaration typically needs a meaningful description rather than a vague word such as gift or accessories. The item’s true quantity, value and country of origin may matter.

A genuine gift does not automatically eliminate duties or paperwork. Do not intentionally undervalue goods or misdescribe their contents to avoid fees.

Keep paperwork consistent

Make sure declared weights and counts are consistent with the packed shipment and any invoice or supporting document. Preserve relevant proof of purchase where applicable.

Requirements such as tariff classifications can be specialized. Consult the carrier or customs authority for the applicable country and service rather than relying on examples from another jurisdiction.

Plan for longer and more variable handling

Border inspection, transits and temporary storage may affect temperature, humidity and handling. Choose an outer carton and inner protection suited to a less predictable journey.

If the item is temperature-sensitive, perishable or particularly fragile, seek product-specific international shipping methods instead of applying an ordinary cardboard-box plan.

Verify rules close to dispatch

Rates, taxes, admissibility, postal rules and required data can change. A checklist made several months ago should be rechecked before buying the label.

Use primary government and carrier resources for live requirements. The ParcelProof Carrier Watch Desk explains the types of changes worth monitoring.

FIELD EXAMPLE / 17

Shipping a handmade ceramic item as an online sale

The ceramic needs fragile-item protection, but the declaration also needs an accurate item description and value. Labeling it merely “gift” does not turn a commercial sale into a personal gift or replace destination customs requirements.

Decision checkpoint: Confirm eligibility, exact content descriptions and destination documents separately from physical packaging.

Before you seal the box

  • Verify item admissibility in both countries.
  • Use an accurate content and value description.
  • Check classification and documents if required.
  • Pack for a longer handling chain.
  • Recheck rules before buying postage.

Common questions

Are gifts exempt from customs paperwork?

Not automatically. Rules depend on the countries, item and value, and customs may still require declarations.

Can this website tell me the correct tariff code?

No. Classification and local import requirements require accurate current information from official sources.
