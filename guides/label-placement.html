Field guide 09 / Shipping prep

Where to place a shipping label on a box

Prevent scanning problems by choosing a flat surface, removing old codes and protecting print readability.

The carrier label is part of the shipment’s operating system. If it is wrinkled, covered, distorted or placed over a seam, a well-packed parcel can become harder to process.

ParcelProof EditorialReviewed 11 Nov 2026Independent educational guide

Choose the largest clean, flat face

A broad box face allows the label to lie flat with no fold through the barcode. The top may be convenient, but use the carrier’s instructions rather than relying on a universal orientation.

Avoid corners, end flaps and areas that bend sharply when the box is handled. Leave room for any service-specific required marks.

Remove outdated labels and barcodes

Reused cartons may carry old address labels, warehouse barcodes or hazard marks. Remove or thoroughly obscure them so the new routing information is unambiguous.

A faded old tracking barcode that remains visible can be more than a cosmetic issue. Inspect all sides, especially the bottom, before applying a new label.

Protect print quality, not just adhesive

Print at the correct scale on a label medium the carrier supports. Ensure barcode bars and quiet zones remain clear, not stretched or clipped by printer settings.

Do not draw over barcode lines. Avoid glossy tape across barcodes unless the carrier explicitly allows it, because glare or wrinkles may interfere with scanning.

Verify after the package is sealed

Wait until the final carton is taped and measured, then attach the label. If the box bulges, a previously flat label can become curved or strained.

Confirm the label matches this parcel, service, destination and weight. A correct box with the wrong address label is still incorrectly prepared.

Keep an internal backup contact card

USPS and UPS recommend an extra address record inside the package in case the exterior label is damaged. Use only the information necessary for return and delivery.

Keep private customer data inside the package, not scattered across external decorative stickers. Treat any old personal identifiers as sensitive and remove them when reusing cartons.

FIELD EXAMPLE / 09

Reusing a shipping carton with three old labels

Covering only the previous address is insufficient if two older barcodes remain exposed. Remove all obsolete machine-readable marks, inspect each face, close the carton, then apply one correct new label flat.

Decision checkpoint: Choose a flat, uninterrupted surface and eliminate obsolete routing information before posting the parcel.

Before you seal the box

  • Check all faces for old barcodes.
  • Complete and close the box first.
  • Use a sharp, correctly scaled print.
  • Keep label off edges and seams.
  • Verify recipient and service details.

Common questions

Can I put a label over packing tape?

Only where the carrier supports the chosen method. Avoid wrinkles, glare and raised seams under the barcode.

What if a reused box has printed brand graphics?

Branding is generally less concerning than conflicting barcodes or addresses. Remove or obscure old shipping marks.
