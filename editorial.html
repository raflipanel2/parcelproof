Publication information

Editorial standards

We write guides around a real action: choosing an outer box, cushioning a fragile item, estimating dimensional weight or verifying a shipping label. We prioritize first-party shipping references from carriers and postal services when stating a rule. We distinguish that rule from our own plain-language examples.

Sources and evidence

We consult public documentation from USPS, UPS and FedEx and provide direct source links when practical. Descriptions of specific packaging materials are educational summaries rather than endorsement or lab testing. Carrier service conditions, fees and hazardous materials restrictions may change. Readers should check current official instructions for the specific shipment before relying on a guide.

Calculator limitations

The Box Fit Planner checks rectangular physical dimensions and allows axis rotations; it does not certify cushioning performance or carton strength. The Dimensional Weight Lab requires a divisor and rounding rule provided by the reader; it does not quote price or fees. The Checklist Builder is a self-audit, not proof of compliance or delivery.

Changes and corrections

Guide publication and review dates represent actual editorial updates, not automated timestamps. We revise pages when we can substantiate a material change. If you see an inaccurate or expired source, send the relevant URL and correction to raflipanel2@gmail.com.

Independence

No articles in this initial edition are sponsored and no specific shipping brand is recommended in exchange for payment. The site may use Google AdSense advertising if approved; ads are separate from editorial decisions. See our advertising disclosure.
