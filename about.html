Publication information

About ParcelProof

ParcelProof is a small independent educational publishing project focused on the moments before someone hands over a parcel: choosing a box, protecting its contents, checking dimensions and preparing a readable label. It does not sell shipping labels, accept parcel orders, represent carriers or receive packages.

Who publishes this website?

ParcelProof is maintained as an independently authored guide library. Our current editorial contact is raflipanel2@gmail.com. We do not claim firsthand testing of every packaging product or carrier route, and we do not claim that our articles have been reviewed or certified by any shipping company.

What makes a guide useful?

A useful guide answers one specific question, gives measurements or an observable test, and explains when that answer stops being reliable. For example, box fit can be checked geometrically, but whether cushioning will survive a particular impact is a more demanding packaging-engineering question.

Where we draw the line

We do not quote live shipping rates, certify packaging, approve dangerous-goods shipments, give customs or legal advice, or guarantee delivery. We send readers to official service documents where restrictions or prices may vary. Our editorial method and source library provide additional detail.
