Tool 01 / physical fit

Box Fit Planner

Compare an item's outer measurements with a box's usable inside dimensions—including cushioning on all six sides.

01 / Item dimensions (cm)

02 / Cushioning and box

Enter the dimensions to test whether the object and its cushioning fit within the usable box interior.

Important: this is a geometric fit check, not a certification of box strength or impact protection. Some shapes cannot safely be rotated. Cushion thickness needs to match the item's fragility and carrier instructions.

How to use the output

Measure the object at its widest points, including retail packaging, protruding handles, or an inner protective box. Then measure your shipping carton internally; sellers sometimes list outside dimensions for shipping prices and inside dimensions for product fit. The planner tests all six axis rotations, but it cannot judge orientation-sensitive items or account for nonrectangular shapes.

For fragile goods, use a cushioning specification appropriate to the object and transit conditions. Our box-size guide and cushioning guide explain the tradeoffs.
