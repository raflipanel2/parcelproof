How to audit your parcel

Start with the product and the route. Packing an unbreakable sweater requires different handling than shipping a glass ornament. The checklist is only useful if its steps are performed, not merely checked off. Keep fragile contents separated from box walls, secure loose pieces and verify the label is readable on a flat face.
