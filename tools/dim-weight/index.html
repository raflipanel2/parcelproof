Parcel's outer size (cm)

Carrier-specific assumptions

Dimensional weight is based on package volume. Actual carrier billing can add minimums, fees and different rounding rules.

The prefilled divisor 5000 is only an illustrative metric example, not a universal carrier rate. Some services use different divisors or units. Check your rate agreement before relying on the result.

Why size can matter more than scale weight

A large parcel of lightweight cushions may take up more space than a compact dense parcel. Many parcel services calculate dimensional weight from exterior length × width × height and compare it with actual scale weight. The higher figure may drive the base rating weight. Incorrect dimensions can cause adjustments.
